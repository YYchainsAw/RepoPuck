# 社区行为准则 / Community Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our pledge

我们希望 RepoPuck 社区对所有人都友好、安全且有建设性，不因年龄、身体状况、残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济状况、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向而区别对待。

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive RepoPuck community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

参与者、贡献者和维护者共同负责营造一个开放、尊重、无骚扰的环境。

Participants, contributors, and maintainers share responsibility for an open, respectful, harassment-free environment.

## 值得鼓励的行为 / Expected behavior

- 尊重不同经验、观点和语言能力；
- 围绕事实、用户影响和可验证结果讨论；
- 提供具体、可执行且友善的反馈；
- 承认错误，愿意修正，并尊重维护者对范围和安全边界的决定；
- 在分享截图、日志和仓库信息前保护他人隐私；
- 优先考虑社区和产品的长期健康。

- Respect different levels of experience, viewpoints, and language proficiency.
- Discuss facts, user impact, and verifiable outcomes.
- Give specific, actionable, and considerate feedback.
- Acknowledge mistakes, make corrections, and respect maintainer decisions about scope and safety.
- Protect other people's privacy before sharing captures, logs, or repository information.
- Prioritize the long-term health of the community and product.

## 不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable behavior

- 性化语言或图像、不受欢迎的性关注或骚扰；
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬损、持续纠缠、人身或政治攻击；
- 公开或私下骚扰、威胁、跟踪以及煽动他人参与；
- 未经明确许可发布住址、邮箱、凭据、私有仓库内容等私人信息；
- 垃圾信息、欺骗性推广、操纵 Star/投票、恶意提交或故意破坏讨论；
- 其他在专业协作环境中明显不恰当的行为。

- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or harassment.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, sustained disruption, or personal and political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, threats, stalking, or encouraging others to participate.
- Publishing private information—such as addresses, email, credentials, or private repository content—without explicit permission.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, manipulation of stars or votes, malicious submissions, or deliberate disruption.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 适用范围 / Scope

本准则适用于仓库、Issue、Pull Request、Discussion、发布渠道，以及任何个人代表 RepoPuck 社区或项目发言的场合。项目标志、官方账号或维护者身份不得被用于骚扰、误导或冒充。

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and whenever someone officially represents RepoPuck. Project marks, official accounts, and maintainer status must not be used for harassment, deception, or impersonation.

## 举报与执行 / Reporting and enforcement

如遇到不当行为，请通过仓库维护者 [YYchainsAw 的 GitHub 资料页](https://github.com/YYchainsAw)中公开的私密联系方式报告。若内容本身违反 GitHub 政策，也可以使用 GitHub 的内容举报功能。

Report unacceptable behavior privately using a contact method published on maintainer [YYchainsAw's GitHub profile](https://github.com/YYchainsAw). Content that violates GitHub policy may also be reported through GitHub's content-reporting tools.

**请勿在公开 Issue 中披露举报者、当事人或事件的敏感信息。** 所有报告将按必要知情原则审查。维护者会尽力保护举报者隐私，但无法对第三方平台或法律要求作出绝对保密承诺。

**Do not disclose sensitive details about reporters, involved parties, or incidents in a public issue.** Reports will be reviewed on a need-to-know basis. Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect reporter privacy but cannot guarantee confidentiality against third-party platform or legal requirements.

维护者可以根据行为的影响、持续时间和历史采取以下措施：

1. **提醒**：私下说明问题和期望的改正；
2. **警告**：要求停止行为，并规定继续参与的条件；
3. **临时限制**：在一定时间内限制互动或贡献；
4. **永久移除**：对持续骚扰、威胁、隐私侵犯或其他严重行为永久封禁。

Depending on impact, duration, and history, maintainers may issue:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation and requested change;
2. **Warning** — a requirement to stop, with conditions for continued participation;
3. **Temporary restriction** — time-limited restrictions on interaction or contribution;
4. **Permanent removal** — a ban for sustained harassment, threats, privacy violations, or other severe conduct.

对行为准则的执行决定由维护者负责；直接涉及事件的维护者应回避相关决定。善意提出不同意见不会因观点本身受到处罚，但表达方式仍须遵守本准则。

Maintainers are responsible for enforcement and should recuse themselves when directly involved in an incident. Good-faith disagreement will not be penalized for its viewpoint, but its expression must still follow this code.
